title: "FilaTwin: A Rotating Digital Preview for Multi-Material 3D Printing"
description: "FilaTwin generates realistic 3D previews of multi-material prints before fabrication, bridging the gap between design intent and printed appearance. It uses vision-language models to analyze photos of previous prints and extract material properties — surface finish, translucency, color gradients, and metallic characteristics — then synthesizes them into procedural shaders for accurate real-time rendering. Implemented as a Blender plugin, FilaTwin introduces an appearance-driven pre-print workflow, letting users see what they will get before printing."
