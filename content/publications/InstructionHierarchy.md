abstract: We investigate how instruction-tuned language models handle conflicting directives
  between system and user inputs. We develop a benchmark with 41 paired constraints
  and test eight models across different conditions, revealing three behavioral categories
  based on system authority response. Despite behavioral variation, the conflict outcome
  is linearly decodable from residual-stream activations at 0.97 balanced accuracy.
  Through targeted steering interventions, we increase system compliance from approximately
  13% to 53%, demonstrating that internal conflict-resolution mechanisms exist even
  when models behaviorally prefer user instructions.
